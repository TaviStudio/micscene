2. Your content and lawful use

MicScene: Voice-over provides Full Track and Segments Voice-over modes. After recording, Full Track adjusts overall Voice and Original levels; Segments adjusts local levels at different positions. The modes are separate: only the selected mode is used for playback, saving, and export. Switching modes resets mix adjustments, including saved adjustments, after confirmation if changes exist. Your voice recording is kept. With no mix adjustments, switching is immediate. Reset Mix clears adjustments in the current mode after confirmation, keeping your voice recording. Resetting or switching modes stops playback and returns to the beginning. Previously saved Library mixes are updated only when you tap Save. Recording and mixing take place on your device. Saving creates or updates a separate Library mix while preserving the imported original. Exporting creates a separate output file. Video Format is stored per Library video and with local editable sessions. Original keeps the source frame; 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, and 16:9 offer Fit with black or blurred background, or Fill with centered cropping. New formats re-encode the picture up to 1080p, preserving the source color profile and SDR or HDR when supported. Video format metadata syncs with Library; editable sessions remain local.

You remain responsible for the audio, video, recordings, Voice-over mixes, and titles you add, create, or export. Use content only as permitted by applicable law. 3. Free plan and Lifetime Pro

Library has no item limit and saving Voice-over mixes to Library is free, subject to available device storage and normal technical limits. The free plan includes 10 successful Voice-over exports on this device. Saving a mix to Library does not count as an export. Successful Voice-over sharing or saving to Files through the share sheet also counts as an export; exports of other saved Library media do not use this allowance. Studio video exports before recording a voice retain the original soundtrack and selected Format and do not use this allowance. Cancelled or failed exports do not use a free export. Deleting items or sessions does not reset the export count. Lifetime Pro removes the Voice-over export limit through a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Library access does not require Lifetime Pro. The price and applicable taxes are shown by Apple before purchase and may vary by storefront. Purchases are processed by Apple and are subject to Apple’s applicable terms and policies. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account to restore an eligible purchase.

On the free plan, playback of Voice-over mixes is unavailable while iOS detects active screen recording or screen mirroring. This restriction applies in Voice-over, Full Screen, Library, Recent Sessions, and video Format previews. Playback pauses without deleting your recordings, saved mixes, or edits. Stop screen capture, then press Play to continue. Original media without an added Voice-over and still screenshots are not affected by this restriction. The restriction does not apply with Lifetime Pro.

4. iCloud Library

iCloud sync is optional and on by default unless your previously saved choice is off. You can enable or disable it in iCloud Library, using the cloud button on the Home screen. Your sync choice is saved separately in your private iCloud database, including when library sync is off. A changed choice must reach iCloud before it can be restored after reinstalling or changing devices. When enabled and available, it syncs your Library, imported media, recordings, and saved Voice-over mixes to your private iCloud storage. Editable sessions in Recent Sessions stay on this device and are not included in iCloud Library sync or restored through it. The free Voice-over export count is also local to the device. iCloud sync requires an internet connection and sufficient iCloud storage. After reinstalling or changing devices, use the same iCloud account. The app applies your last sync choice saved in iCloud. If it was on, previously synced data downloads automatically while the app is open; if it was off, it stays off until you enable it. If iCloud is unavailable, your local Library remains available and library transfers wait. Only data that finished syncing can be restored. Turning sync off preserves local data and existing iCloud copies; deletions also sync while enabled. iCloud sync does not provide a version history or guarantee recovery of unsynced or deleted items.

Sync may continue for a limited time after you leave the app or lock the device, and iOS may schedule later background sync opportunities. These opportunities are not continuous or guaranteed. Interrupted transfers resume on a later permitted run; an incomplete file may need to be transferred again.

Before uninstalling the app or switching devices, check that Library syncing has finished and keep separate copies of important files. Editable sessions in Recent Sessions remain local and are removed when the app is uninstalled. Saving or exporting a final mix preserves the combined result, not the editable session. MicScene: Voice-over does not guarantee data recovery, including in the event of iCloud issues or app errors.
