2. Your content and lawful use

MicScene: Voice-over provides Full Track and Segments Voice-over modes. After recording, Full Track adjusts overall Voice and Original levels; Segments adjusts local levels at different positions. The modes are separate: only the selected mode is used for playback, saving, and export. Switching modes resets mix adjustments, including saved adjustments, after confirmation if changes exist. Your voice recording is kept. With no mix adjustments, switching is immediate. Reset Mix clears adjustments in the current mode after confirmation, keeping your voice recording. Resetting or switching modes stops playback and returns to the beginning. Previously saved Library mixes are updated only when you tap Save. Recording and mixing take place on your device. When a Voice-over recording stops or is interrupted, MicScene attempts to keep a recovery copy in Sessions. Tapping Save keeps the editable project in Sessions and creates or updates a separate, playable Voice-over mix in Library. If the original source is already in Library, it remains a separate item. Exporting creates a separate output file. Video Format is stored per Library video and with local editable sessions. Original keeps the source frame; 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, and 16:9 offer Fit with black or blurred background, or Fill with centered cropping. New formats re-encode the picture up to 1080p, preserving the source color profile and SDR or HDR when supported. Video format metadata syncs with the video or editable Session when iCloud Sync is enabled.

You remain responsible for the audio, video, recordings, Voice-over mixes, and titles you add, create, or export. Use content only as permitted by applicable law. 3. Free plan and Lifetime Pro

Library holds up to 50 items, and Sessions holds up to 50 projects. Local imports and saves in both sections share a 5 GB on-device limit for saved media. These limits apply to all plans, subject to available device storage and normal technical limits. You can remove items or projects to make room. Each distinct editable project counts toward the Sessions limit, even when several projects use the same source. A project and its recovery copies share one Sessions slot. An original source and a saved mix count as separate Library items when both are in Library. Saving a Voice-over mix to Library is free. The free plan includes 10 successful Voice-over exports on this device. Saving a mix to Library does not count as an export. Successful Voice-over sharing or saving to Files through the share sheet also counts as an export; exports of other saved Library media do not use this allowance. Cancelled or failed exports do not use a free export. Deleting items or sessions does not reset the export count. Lifetime Pro removes the Voice-over export limit through a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Library access does not require Lifetime Pro. The price and applicable taxes are shown by Apple before purchase and may vary by storefront. Purchases are processed by Apple and are subject to Apple’s applicable terms and policies. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account to restore an eligible purchase.

On the free plan, playback of Voice-over mixes is unavailable while iOS detects active screen recording or screen mirroring. This restriction applies in Voice-over, Full Screen, Library, Sessions, and video Format previews. Playback pauses without deleting your recordings, saved mixes, or edits. Stop screen capture, then press Play to continue. Original media without an added Voice-over and still screenshots are not affected by this restriction. The restriction does not apply with Lifetime Pro.

4. iCloud Sync

iCloud sync is off by default. Use the cloud button on Home to change your preference. MicScene stores the preference separately in your private iCloud database, including when content sync is off. Once a changed choice reaches iCloud, it can be restored to devices on the same iCloud account. When enabled and available, iCloud sync includes Library and editable Sessions, including the source, recorded voice, and saved mix and video format. The free Voice-over export count stays on the device. Sync requires internet access and sufficient iCloud space. On another device or after reinstalling, only completed transfers can be restored. Turning sync off preserves local data and existing iCloud copies. Deletions of Library items and Sessions sync while enabled. Shared media can remain in private iCloud storage after deletion. You can manage that storage in device Settings. Sync does not provide a version history.

Previously synced Library items and editable Sessions can be restored even if they bring the combined on-device storage above 5 GB. Restored media counts toward the storage limit for future local imports and saves. If an import or save would increase storage use above the limit, remove enough saved media to make room.

Sync may continue for a limited time after you leave the app or lock the device, and iOS may schedule later background sync opportunities. These opportunities are not continuous or guaranteed. Interrupted transfers resume on a later permitted run; an incomplete file may need to be transferred again.

Before uninstalling the app or switching devices, check that syncing has finished and keep separate copies of important files. The export file contains the mixed result. For an editable copy, confirm the Session itself has completed iCloud sync. MicScene: Voice-over does not guarantee data recovery, including in the event of iCloud issues or app errors.
