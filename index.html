One voice. Two ways to mix.

Make room for your voice.

Choose an overall balance or shape the sound at different points in your media.

Full Track

A consistent balance.

Adjust Voice and Original levels across the entire track. Keep your narration clear while the original audio sets the mood.

Segments

Control the moment.

Adjust local Voice and Original levels at different positions. Bring the original sound forward or give your voice more space.

Video Format

Choose your frame.

Choose and preview a video format before recording, or keep the original frame. Fit with a black or blurred background, or Fill with centered cropping.

Record Audio

Make a standalone recording.

Capture audio with the microphone. Listen back, record again if needed, then save it to Library. You can use it as a Voice-over source later.

Record Video

Film directly in MicScene.

Use your device’s camera to capture a clip. Keep it in Library or start a Voice-over with it from New Session.

Your Library

Keep your creations together.

Open a Library item’s menu and choose Preview to listen or watch before editing. Save mixes to Library and reopen your editable voice-over projects in Sessions. Turn on iCloud sync to keep your Library and saved mixes in sync. Editable sessions stay on this device, and iCloud sync is off by default.
