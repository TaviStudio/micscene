Privacy Policy

Your files stay yours

MicScene stores your imported media, recordings, Voice-over sessions, and saved mixes on your device. Recording, mixing, and file processing take place locally. When Sync with iCloud is enabled and iCloud is available, your Library and editable Sessions also sync to your private iCloud storage.

Works Offline

Import media available on your device, record your voice over audio or video, adjust and save Voice-over mixes, organize your Library, and play locally saved media without an internet connection. Syncing with iCloud and purchasing or restoring Lifetime Pro require an internet connection. Media stored only in Files providers or iCloud Photos must first be downloaded.

No Ads or Tracking

MicScene contains no ads and does not use tracking or usage analytics. No separate app account is required.

Local Files

Choose audio or compatible video from Files, or select compatible videos from Gallery. Selected media is copied into the app's private storage. Compatible audio and video are copied without re-encoding. Audio is converted locally to M4A/AAC only when needed for compatibility. Library items, including saved Voice-over mixes and their titles, also sync through iCloud when enabled and available. MicScene uses Dark appearance. Playback preferences remain on your device.

Gallery Access

Gallery uses Apple’s video picker. The app receives only videos you select and does not request access to your entire photo library. If a selected video is stored only in iCloud Photos, Apple must download it before import can finish.

iCloud Sync

When enabled, MicScene syncs your Library and editable Sessions to the private iCloud database associated with your iCloud account. This includes imported media, saved mixes, recorded voice, and the saved mix settings and video format of editable Sessions. This uses your iCloud storage and requires an internet connection. Local copies remain available for offline playback and editing. Your free Voice-over export count stays on this device.

On another device or after reinstalling, sign in to the same iCloud account to restore completed Library and Session transfers. If your saved choice was off, sync stays off until you enable it. This sync is separate from any device backup you enable in iOS Settings.

iCloud sync is off by default. MicScene stores your most recent choice separately in your private iCloud database, including when content sync is off. A changed choice must reach iCloud before other devices can retrieve it.

If iCloud is unavailable, your local files and Sessions remain available while transfers wait. Use the cloud button on Home to turn sync on or off or to check its status. Turning it off preserves local files and existing iCloud copies. Deletions sync while it is enabled.

If MicScene detects that its app data was removed from iCloud, it pauses sync and keeps the files still on your device. To upload your local Library and editable Sessions again, choose Resume iCloud Sync and confirm Resume and Upload while signed in to the same iCloud account.

iCloud sync may continue for a limited time after you leave the app or lock your device. iOS may also schedule later background sync opportunities. Timing and completion depend on system conditions. Interrupted sync can resume when you return or iOS allows another run.

Camera, Microphone and Recordings

The microphone is accessed only with your permission when you use Record Audio, record video with sound, or record your voice in Studio. Record Video also asks for camera permission and uses Apple’s native camera interface. Audio and video are captured and processed locally. Record Audio creates a standalone recording and offers playback and Record Again before saving. Record Video saves the clip you choose to keep to Library. Use Preview in a Library item’s menu to play audio or video without opening the Voice-over editor. Saved recordings and Library mixes also sync through iCloud when enabled and available. You can revoke camera or microphone permission in device Settings at any time. Library import, playback, and export remain available if you decline capture access.

Voice-over Sessions

Voice-over records your voice while the selected audio or video plays, then lets you adjust Voice and Original sound separately. After recording, Full Track adjusts overall levels and Segments adjusts local levels at different positions. Only the selected mode is used for the mix. Switching modes resets mix adjustments while keeping your voice recording. The app asks for confirmation if there are mix adjustments to reset, including saved adjustments. Unchanged levels switch without a confirmation. Reset Mix clears adjustments in the current mode after confirmation, keeping your voice recording. Resetting or switching modes stops playback and returns to the beginning. Previously saved Library mixes are updated only when you tap Save. Saving creates a mixed Library item while preserving the original source; saving the same session again updates that mix. Editable projects appear in Sessions. Stored takes, including recovery copies created when recording stops, can sync with their source, voice, mix and video format. Edits must be saved on the device before they can sync.

Export and Sharing

Export from Library prepares a private copy. With Original format, the saved file is copied without reconverting it. A selected video Format is applied to preview and export without changing the saved source file. In Voice-over, you can choose and preview a video format before recording. After recording, Export renders the current mix as a separate file and does not update the saved Library mix. Audio mixes offer High Quality (AAC) and Lossless (ALAC) export. With Original format, compatible video is preserved without re-encoding when possible; the mixed audio is rendered separately. Lossless describes the output encoding and does not recover detail missing from a compressed source. The native iOS share sheet lets you save to Files or send the chosen file to a destination you select. The destination app or service handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy.

Apple Services

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles iCloud and its services under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Apple Privacy Policy

Free Voice-over Exports

Saving a mix to Library is free. The free plan includes 10 successful Voice-over exports on this device. Saving a mix does not count as an export. Successful sharing or saving a Voice-over mix to Files through the share sheet counts as an export; exporting other Library media does not. A count of completed Voice-over exports is stored on this device in Keychain and in a private local recovery copy excluded from device backups. The count is separate from Library items and editable sessions and is not reset when you delete a Library mix or a session. Cancelled and failed exports do not count. The count is not used for tracking or analytics and is not synced by MicScene through iCloud. Lifetime Pro removes the Voice-over export limit.

Retention and Deletion

You can delete Library items from the app; deletions also sync when iCloud sync is enabled and available. Deleting a saved Voice-over mix or its Library source can also remove linked editable sessions from Sessions. Their local source and voice copies are cleaned up when Studio is not using them. If the Library source of an open session is removed, the open session can continue with its own local source copy. When recording stops or is interrupted, MicScene attempts to keep a recovery copy in Recent Sessions. Recovery copies can sync when iCloud Sync is on. You can also use Delete Session in Sessions to remove an editable session and its local files. Session deletions also sync when enabled. Shared media can remain in your private iCloud storage after a project is deleted. You can manage the app’s iCloud storage in device Settings. Other Library items and files you exported are kept. If file cleanup fails, the app retains the cleanup request and offers Retry Cleanup. Uninstalling removes locally stored sessions and the app container according to the operating system's behavior. Only completed iCloud copies of Library items or Sessions can be restored. Files you exported are managed separately in their destination app or service.
