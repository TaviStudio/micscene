Privacy Policy

Your files stay yours

Your imported media, recordings, Voice-over sessions, and saved mixes are stored on your device. Recording, mixing, and file processing take place locally. When Sync with iCloud is enabled and iCloud is available, items saved in Library also sync to your private iCloud storage.

Works Offline

Import media available on your device, record your voice over audio or video, adjust and save Voice-over mixes, organize your Library, and play locally saved media without an internet connection. Syncing with iCloud and purchasing or restoring Lifetime Pro require an internet connection. Media stored only in Files providers or iCloud Photos must first be downloaded.

No Ads or Tracking

MicScene: Voice-over contains no ads and does not use tracking or usage analytics. No separate app account is required.

Local Files

Choose audio or compatible video from Files, or select compatible videos from Gallery. Selected media is copied into the app's private storage. Compatible audio and video are copied without re-encoding. Audio is converted locally to M4A/AAC only when needed for compatibility. Library items, including saved Voice-over mixes and their titles, also sync through iCloud when enabled and available. MicScene: Voice-over uses Dark appearance. Playback preferences remain on your device.

Gallery Access

Gallery uses Apple’s video picker. The app receives only videos you select and does not request access to your entire photo library. If a selected video is stored only in iCloud Photos, Apple must download it before import can finish.

iCloud Library

Library data, imported audio and video, recordings, and saved Voice-over mixes sync to your private iCloud database, associated with the iCloud account signed in on your device. This uses your iCloud storage and requires an internet connection. A local copy remains available for offline playback.

Editable Voice-over sessions in Recent Sessions, including their source and voice files, stay on this device. They are not synced or restored through iCloud Library. The free Voice-over export count is also local to this device.

After reinstalling or changing devices, sign in to the same iCloud account. The app restores your last sync choice saved in iCloud: if it was on, your synced Library downloads automatically while the app is open; if it was off, it stays off until you enable it in iCloud Library, using the cloud button on the Home screen. Only changes that finished syncing can be restored.

Sync with iCloud is on by default unless your previously saved choice is off. Your sync choice is stored separately in your private iCloud database, including when Library sync is off, so it can be remembered after reinstalling or changing devices. A changed choice must reach iCloud before it can be restored elsewhere.

If iCloud is unavailable, your local Library remains available and Library transfers wait. You can turn sync on or off and check its status in iCloud Library, using the cloud button on the Home screen. Turning it off stops syncing without deleting the local Library or copies already saved in iCloud. Deletions also sync while it is enabled.

iCloud sync may continue for a limited time after you leave the app or lock your device. iOS may also schedule later background sync opportunities. Timing and completion depend on system conditions. Interrupted sync resumes when you return or iOS allows another run.

Microphone and Recordings

The microphone is accessed only with your permission when you record your voice in Studio. Audio is captured and processed locally. Saved Library mixes also sync through iCloud when enabled and available. You can revoke microphone permission in device Settings at any time. Library import, playback, and export remain available if you decline microphone access.

Voice-over Sessions

Voice-over records your voice while the selected audio or video plays, then lets you adjust Voice and Original sound separately. After recording, Full Track adjusts overall levels and Segments adjusts local levels at different positions. Only the selected mode is used for the mix. Switching modes resets mix adjustments while keeping your voice recording. The app asks for confirmation if there are mix adjustments to reset, including saved adjustments. Unchanged levels switch without a confirmation. Reset Mix clears adjustments in the current mode after confirmation, keeping your voice recording. Resetting or switching modes stops playback and returns to the beginning. Previously saved Library mixes are updated only when you tap Save. Saving creates a mixed Library item while preserving the original source; saving the same session again updates that mix. Editable sessions and their source and voice files stay on this device until removed through the session or Library deletion controls. Available sessions appear in Recent Sessions. They are not included in iCloud Library sync or restored through it.

Export and Sharing

Export from Library prepares a private copy. With Original format, the saved file is copied without reconverting it. A selected video Format is applied to preview and export without changing the saved source file. Before recording a voice in Studio, you can export a video with its original soundtrack and selected Format without using a free Voice-over export. After recording, Export renders the current mix as a separate file and does not update the saved Library mix. Audio mixes offer High Quality (AAC) and Lossless (ALAC) export. With Original format, compatible video is preserved without re-encoding when possible; the mixed audio is rendered separately. Lossless describes the output encoding and does not recover detail missing from a compressed source. The native iOS share sheet lets you save to Files or send the chosen file to a destination you select. The destination app or service handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy.

Apple Services

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles iCloud and its services under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Apple Privacy Policy

Free Voice-over Exports

Library has no item limit and saving mixes to Library is free, subject to available device storage. The free plan includes 10 successful Voice-over exports on this device. Saving a mix does not count as an export. Successful sharing or saving a Voice-over mix to Files through the share sheet counts as an export; exporting other Library media does not. A count of completed Voice-over exports is stored on this device in Keychain and in a private local recovery copy excluded from device backups. The count is separate from Library items and editable sessions and is not reset when you delete a Library mix or a session. Cancelled and failed exports do not count. The count is not used for tracking or analytics and is not synced through iCloud Library. Lifetime Pro removes the Voice-over export limit.

Retention and Deletion

You can delete Library items from the app; deletions also sync when iCloud sync is enabled and available. Deleting a saved Voice-over mix or its Library source removes associated editable sessions from Recent. Their local source and voice copies are cleaned up when Studio is not using them. A session already open in Studio is kept while you work. You can also use Delete Session in Recent Sessions to remove an editable session and its local files. Other Library items and files you exported are kept. If file cleanup fails, the app retains the cleanup request and offers Retry Cleanup. Uninstalling removes locally stored sessions and the app container according to the operating system's behavior. Only completed iCloud Library copies can be restored. Files you exported are managed separately in their destination app or service. You can manage the app's iCloud storage in device Settings.
